Our story

I have always been the friend people ask, “Do you know someone?”

Most of the time, I say no.

Introducing two friends is not the same as sending them another profile. If it goes badly, they both remember that you put them in the room. Your judgment and your reputation are part of the introduction.

That makes you careful.

Being single is not enough. Looking good on paper is not enough. I only introduce two people when I genuinely believe they should meet, because I still have to face both of them afterwards.

Donna works the same way.

There is no endless list and no introduction made just to keep you interested. I read every application and put my name behind every match.

Some weeks, I may not have anyone for you. I would rather tell you that than introduce you to someone I would not introduce to a friend.

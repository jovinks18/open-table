Safety at donna

What we review, what we cannot guarantee, and how you stay in control.

Emergency help

Application review

Every application is reviewed

We read every application ourselves and check that what someone's told us is consistent.

Permission

Nothing is shared without permission

Your profile is only shared for a specific introduction, and only after you agree.

First meetings

Meet somewhere public

Choose a populated place, arrange your own transport, and tell someone you trust.

During the meeting

We know when and where you're meeting

We arrange the time and place, so we know where you'll be. We'll message you afterwards to check you got home fine. If you'd rather we didn't, just say.

Your choice

You stay in control

You can decline, leave, stop communicating, or withdraw from donna at any time.

Money

Money requests are a warning sign

Never send money or financial information to someone we introduce you to.

Reporting

Tell us when something feels wrong

You do not need to prove it was serious before reporting a concern.

What happens next

What we do about it

We reply within 2 hours. You'll speak to one person, not a queue. If someone has behaved badly, we stop introducing them.

Report a concern

Message us on WhatsApp at +1 3413338019 or email thedonnapilot@gmail.com. Jovin reads these. We reply within 2 hours.

You don't need to prove it was serious before telling us.

If you're in immediate danger, call 112.

Women's helpline: 1091 · Women in distress: 181 · Cyber and financial fraud: 1930

donna is not an emergency service.

Manual review can reduce avoidable risk, but it cannot guarantee another person’s identity, intentions or behaviour. If you believe you are in immediate danger, contact the appropriate local emergency services. donna is not an emergency-response service.

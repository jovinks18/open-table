Safety at donna

What we do, what we can’t do, and what to do if something goes wrong.

We verify every member, arrange first meetings in public, and listen before and after every introduction. That reduces avoidable risk. It doesn’t remove it, and this page is honest about the difference.

Your safety playbook

Before you meet When you meet After you meet If something happens

Before you meet

We check what we reasonably can.

Before making an introduction, we confirm identity and age, review the application, and check the stated basics of someone’s work and situation.

What is shared

Only enough information to consider the introduction. Contact details are shared after you meet, if you both want them.

Money

We will never ask you for money. Neither should anyone we introduce you to. If someone does, stop and tell us.

What checks cannot prove

Verification can establish that someone is real. It cannot guarantee their intentions, judgment or future behaviour.

When you meet

The first meeting stays public.

We arrange a time and a public venue. Travel independently, tell someone you trust where you are going, and keep control of when and how you leave.

You never owe anyone more time, attention or politeness because donna arranged the meeting.

After you meet

Tell us what actually happened.

Your debrief is private. We use it to improve future introductions and to notice behaviour that may mean someone should not be introduced again.

The other person does not receive your debrief.

If something happens

You do not need to prove it was serious first.

Leave whenever you need to. Save messages or details that may help, tell someone you trust, and report the concern to us when you are able.

Our non-negotiables

Consent · Respect · No harassment · No money requests

These aren’t guidelines. Break them and you won’t be introduced to anyone again.

Need help now?

Immediate danger — call 112.

Harassment, stalking, or feeling unsafe — call 181 or 1091.

Financial fraud, blackmail, or online abuse — call 1930, or use the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.

Manual review and safety checks can reduce risk, but they cannot guarantee another person’s identity, intentions or behaviour. donna is not an emergency service.

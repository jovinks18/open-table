Safety at Donna

We take precautions before, during and after an introduction. Here is what that means in practice.

Every application is reviewed

Every application is read by a person before an introduction is made. We look for inconsistencies and may ask for more information when something does not add up.

Your profile is not public

Your profile and photos are only shown to someone being considered for a specific introduction. There is no searchable directory, and your full application is never shared.

First meetings happen in public

Donna arranges the time and a populated public venue. Use your own transport and tell someone you trust where you are going. We will message you after the meeting to check that you got home safely.

You stay in control

You can decline an introduction, leave a meeting, stop communicating or withdraw from Donna at any time. Never send money or financial information to someone you meet through Donna.

Something feels wrong? Tell us.

You do not need to prove that something was serious before contacting us. We review safety concerns promptly and may pause further introductions involving the person while we assess what happened.

Emergency help

Donna is not an emergency service. If you are in immediate danger, call 112.

  • Immediate emergency: 112
  • Women’s helpline: 181
  • Cyber or financial fraud: 1930

Application review and safety precautions can reduce risk, but they cannot guarantee another person’s identity, intentions or behaviour.

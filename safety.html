Safety at donna

What donna reviews, what it cannot guarantee, and how you stay in control.

Application review

Every application is reviewed

donna manually reviews applications and checks submitted information for consistency.

Permission

Nothing is shared without permission

Your profile is only shared for a specific introduction, and only after you agree.

First meetings

Meet somewhere public

Choose a populated place, arrange your own transport, and tell someone you trust.

Your choice

You stay in control

You can decline, leave, stop communicating, or withdraw from donna at any time.

Money

Money requests are a warning sign

Never send money or financial information to someone donna introduces you to.

Reporting

Tell us when something feels wrong

You do not need to prove it was serious before reporting a concern.

Support

Report a concern

Tell donna about concerning behaviour, decline an introduction, or ask for help leaving the pilot.

Contact donna donna’s safety contact is being configured.

Manual review can reduce avoidable risk, but it cannot guarantee another person’s identity, intentions or behaviour. If you believe you are in immediate danger, contact the appropriate local emergency services. donna is not an emergency-response service.

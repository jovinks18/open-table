FAQ

What is Donna?

Donna is a small, human-led matchmaking service. There is nothing to swipe through and no list of people to browse. You apply, we get to know you, and a person decides each introduction.

Who can apply?

Donna is for people aged 21 and over in Bangalore who are looking for marriage or a serious relationship that could lead there. If you are looking for something casual, this is not the right place.

How does an introduction work?

When we find someone, we show each of you the other person’s profile and photos. You decide privately. If you both say yes, Donna makes the introduction and arranges the first meeting at a vetted public venue.

What will the other person see about me?

Only the profile information and photos needed to consider the introduction. Your full application, private preferences and matchmaker notes are not shared.

Do you consider family, religion, community or caste?

Only if you tell us they matter to you. You decide where you are flexible and where you are not. Caste information, if provided, is kept for the matchmaker and is not automatically shown to another person.

How long might I wait?

There is no fixed timeline or guaranteed number of introductions. Donna makes one introduction at a time. If we do not have the right person for you, we wait.

What does Donna cost?

Donna is currently free. If that changes, we will tell you before any payment is required.

How is my information used?

We keep the information you submit, including your profile details, preferences, photos and private feedback. Access is limited to the people running Donna. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

You can stop participating or ask us to delete your information at any time. Limited safety or legal records may be retained where necessary, as explained in our Privacy Notice.

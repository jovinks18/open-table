FAQ

The questions people ask before applying.

The basics

Is this a dating app?

No. There’s nothing to download and nothing to browse. We get to know you, then introduce you to one person at a time.

Who is donna, exactly?

A small team, not software. Every application is read by a person, and every introduction is decided by a person.

Who is it for?

People who want to be married and are tired of spending months finding out someone else didn’t. If you’re looking to date casually, this isn’t the right fit.

Who is this not for?

If you want to date casually, this isn't it. Otherwise we're open — we work with people who've been married before, and there's no age limit. The one thing we ask is that you're ready to actually meet someone.

Where are you?

Bangalore, for now. If you’re somewhere else, apply anyway — we’ll tell you when we’re in your city.

How introductions work

How does an introduction actually work?

We get to know you first. When we think of someone you should meet, we describe them to you and describe you to them. If you both say yes, we arrange a time and a public place. You meet there.

Do you match on community or religion?

We ask. For a lot of people it matters, and pretending it doesn't wastes everyone's time. You tell us where you're flexible and where you aren't. We don't decide that for you.

Can my family be involved?

Yes, if you want them to be — a lot of people don't make this decision alone. Tell us how involved you'd like them to be. We won't contact anyone in your family unless you ask us to.

Will I see a photo before we meet?

Once you've both said yes to an introduction, photos are shared before we set a time.

Why don’t I get their number beforehand?

Because three weeks of texting tells you less than forty minutes of coffee, and it costs a lot more. You’ll both have each other’s details after you’ve met, if you want them.

How many introductions will I get?

Fewer than you’re used to. We’d rather make four introductions you’d have said yes to than forty you’d scroll past.

What if they don't show up?

Tell us and we'll sort it out. You won't be left explaining yourself, and it won't count against you.

What if they keep contacting me afterwards and I'd rather they didn't?

Tell us. We'll speak to them, and we won't introduce them to anyone else until it's sorted.

What happens after the meeting?

We ask you both how it went — honestly, including the awkward parts. Not a rating of the other person, just how the meeting was for you. It helps us get the next introduction right.

If something went wrong, that's different. Tell us any time.

Does the other person see my debrief?

No. What you tell us stays with us.

How do you check people are real?

We ask for a government ID and proof of where you work, and we check both before any introduction. That makes it much harder to be someone you're not. It doesn't make it impossible.

Cost and your data

What does it cost?

Nothing while we're piloting. If that changes, you'll hear it from us first.

Then how do you make money?

We don't, yet. We're small and doing this by hand while we learn how to do it well. We don't sell data and we don't run ads. That won't change.

Is my information safe?

Your application stays with us. It isn’t shared with other members, sold, or used for advertising. The people you’re introduced to only learn what you’d be comfortable telling them yourself.

What do you actually store?

Your application, the documents we use to check you're real, and what you tell us after a meeting. We delete the verification documents once we've checked them. Nothing is shared with other members, sold, or used for advertising.

Who can see it?

Only the people on our team who need to. Verification documents are used to confirm you’re real and are not shown to anyone you’re introduced to.

Can I have my data deleted?

Yes. Ask and we'll delete what we hold about you.

One exception: if someone has reported a safety concern about you, we keep a note of it. Deleting that would defeat the point of them telling us. Everything else goes.

Can I stop?

Any time, in one message. No explanation needed and we won’t ask for one.

Manual review and safety checks can reduce risk, but they cannot guarantee another person’s identity, intentions or behaviour.
